The data in [data002.csv](/Code/data/data002.csv) shows how the CO2, temperature, pressure and relative humidity change when moving from an apartment to a parking garage while taking an elevator.  To extend the time in the elevator, we went from the second floor, to the basement, and up to the second floor again before leaving the elevator in the basement.

There were minor changed in the temperature and relative humidity, the CO2 and pressure show more significant changes.
